## 1. Purpose

The purpose of the National September 11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center’s Generative AI Usage policy is to ensure legal and ethical use of Generative AI (GenAI) technology. The guidance below defines acceptable and prohibited uses and outlines employee obligations related to the use or development of GenAI models and applications in connection with their work on behalf of the 9/11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center. 

This policy was developed by the organization’s interdepartmental AI Steering Committee.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who develop, deploy, manage, or use GenAI on behalf of the National September 11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center. This policy applies to the use of those applications on company devices and/or personal devices when used for work purposes.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Any use of GenAI, via platforms, tools, and software must be consistent with our Code of Conduct & Ethics, company policy, and applicable law. Use of GenAI on company devices must be limited to business purposes. Use of GenAI on personal devices to circumvent company policies or safeguards is strictly prohibited.

Individual managers may have concerns about their employees’ use of GenAI and may restrict their employees’ use of GenAI for business purposes. If an individual manager restricts their employees’ use of GenAI, the manager is required to inform the AI Policy Steering Committee by emailing <AIpolicy@911memorial.org>.

Any violation of this policy or use of unauthorized GenAI not listed in the table below or otherwise pre-authorized in writing, will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

Below is information about GenAI that employees may use in connection with their work at the National September 11 Memorial & Museum. The organization reserves the right to make changes to, or update this table periodically:

**Accepted Use**

Employees may use GenAI in circumstances where GenAI assists the GenAI user in performing job-related tasks by enhancing productivity, efficiency, and decision-making. For example, GenAI can be a helpful aid in:

- General research
- Generating outlines, ideas, first drafts of documents and basic correspondence
- Summarizing text from a secondary, publicly available source

- Do not upload sensitive company data or personal information.

Employees must complete a brief onboarding with the IT Department before using GenAI or LinkedIn Learning to ensure proper configuration and data protection (see below for more details on onboarding process).

## 4. Policy Guidelines

For any use of GenAI, employees must adhere to the following: 

4.1 Each employee who uses GenAI is personally accountable for the GenAI output they share with others or rely on for making decisions or taking action. Each employee who uses GenAI is personally responsible for ensuring that the output meets or exceeds the quality standards of the National September 11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center.

4.2 To maintain the **security of our data and IT systems**, employees are prohibited from attempting to gain access to **<u>un</u>**approved GenAI when using company systems or networks, conducting business on behalf of the company, or when using the company’s data.

To avoid potential data leaks or security incidents employees must:

- **Not** install non-approved Application Programming Interfaces (APIs), plug-ins, connectors, or software related to GenAI.

- **Never** implement or use on company systems in any way code generated by GenAI.

- **Complete** a short IT Department Onboarding before using approved/permitted GenAI on company devices. The onboarding was developed to ensure, among other things, that device settings are appropriately adjusted to limit, to the extent possible, unnecessary sharing of National September 11 Memorial & Museum information with GenAI training models. Departing employees are required to complete an offboarding process as well. To request IT Department onboarding before using approved/permitted applications, employees must email: <helpdesk@911memorial.org> Offboarding will be initiated by the IT Department.

4.2 To maintain the **confidentiality of our company’s sensitive information**, including but not limited to employee and customer personal information, our intellectual property, and copyrighted material, internal documentation (plans, memos, charts, policies, contracts etc.) employees and contractors must abide by the following to maintain confidentiality:

- **Do not share** personally identifiable information (PII) and other forms of sensitive or confidential information parties with any GenAI. Treat the GenAI as you would an employee of another company with whom we have no formal relationship.

- **Do** contact your manager or email AIpolicy@911memorial.org if you are unsure whether the information you plan to share with a GenAI falls into any of the above categories.

4.3 To **maintain transparency** with customers and employees and protect the company from **claims against copyright infringement** and/or theft of intellectual property, all GenAI generated content must be cited and reviewed if used for work purposes. To protect the company:

- **Do** clearly attribute any output used for work purposes to the GenAI that created it through a footnote or other means visible to the reader.

- **Do** maintain an updated record of GenAI use for work purposes and be able to share those records with your manager or other authorized company personnel upon request.

4.4 To protect our **employees and clients from harm and to protect the company from reputational damage, employees must use GenAI pursuant to the National September 11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center**’s Code of Conduct & Ethics and Policy. GenAI-created content that is inappropriate, discriminatory, or otherwise harmful to our employees or clients/customers must not be used. Such use will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination. To protect our employees, customers, and the company:

- **Do** review the output of GenAI to make sure it meets the Company’s standards for principles of equity, ethics, and appropriateness.

- **Do not** use any output that discriminates against individuals based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, marital status, political affiliation, or sexual orientation.

- **Do not** use GenAI to create text, audio, or visual content for purposes of committing fraud or misrepresenting an individual’s identity.

- **Do not use GenAI for personnel decision making purposes.**

4.5 The National September 11 Memorial & Museum recognizes that an informed workforce is our best line of defense against misuse of technology, data breaches, misinformation, and other risks that could compromise our mission, operations, or public trust. Training on the responsible use of GenAI will be provided from time to time and such training is required for all users who plan on using GenAI in connection with their work.

4.6 All employees and contractors are expected to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or company policies regarding the use or development of GenAI and GenAI output.

4.7 All employees are expected to report non-compliance with this policy to AIpolicy@911memorial.org or Ethicspoint at <https://secure.ethicspoint.com/domain/media/en/gui/19476/index.html> Employees are encouraged to speak up when they witness misconduct. Employees who report misconduct or concern in good faith will not be retaliated against.

## 5. Monitoring

Any GenAI-related activities which appear to violate applicable laws may be reported to external law enforcement.

If monitoring systems and processes detect a possible policy violation or if a User reports a possible policy violation, the suspect event should be processed using appropriate security incident response processes.

## 10. Appendix

### 10.1. Key Context

**10.1.1 Generative AI (“GenAI”)** is an artificial intelligence technology that synthesizes new versions of text, audio, or visual imagery from large bodies of data in response to user prompts. GenAI incorporates models built from massive datasets that can be used in stand-alone applications like ChatGPT or Bard or incorporated into other applications such as internet search engines or word processing applications.

**10.1.2 Privacy, Security, and Confidentiality:** Information is shared with GenAI by user prompts or a series of instructions or questions for GenAI. Generally, providing access to information constitutes sharing data with GenAI. The sharing of data potentially makes confidential or sensitive information public as the GenAI may train its model on the data shared. In some cases, data that has been anonymized could be linked to personal information and become exposed. Any customer or employee’s personal information, company proprietary information or intellectual property, or otherwise confidential information entered into the prompt may appear in other users’ output. Therefore, users of GenAI should avoid entering any information into a GenAI that they do not want to be made public or is otherwise restricted by law or policy.

**10.1.3 Verification of Generative AI Output:** Outputs created by GenAI tools may provide fictitious answers. These are sometimes referred to as hallucinations. Furthermore, many open source GenAI models are often trained on large, publicly available datasets (e.g., through data extraction of public web pages). The outputs may, therefore, contain copyrighted information or others’ intellectual property. While ownership in many of these cases is unclear, users should err on the side of caution and not use any output that contains material they suspect to be under copyright protection in any materials, internal or external facing. To guard against copyright infringement, employees are expected to review GenAI outputs for originality and ensure compliance with intellectual property standards, following internal guidance and approved review practices.

Users of GenAI must also be aware that GenAI incorporate any biases of the data sets that were used to train them. This modeling bias may not always align with the National September 11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center’s core values and our commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion. Therefore, model output may make systematic errors or favor certain groups, leading to unfair or discriminatory outcomes.

Using output from GenAI without reviewing it for accuracy places the company at risk. It may harm the National September 11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center’s reputation. 

**10.1.4 Transparency:** We aim to provide our employees, third parties, and customers with transparency regarding how we use GenAI to support our work. All content generated using GenAI must be identified on any outward-facing materials where the AI-generated content forms a substantive part of the final product—such as published articles, marketing collateral, press releases, or official reports. Routine internal communications (e.g., emails or meeting notes) do not require attribution unless the content is being externally shared or formally published.

**10.1.5 Third-party risk**: Data sent by National September 11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center to third parties could be used in the third party’s use of GenAI. Uses include but are not limited to, training new GenAI models, providing updated information for existing GenAI models, and improving the user experience. Sensitive information input into unapproved GenAI may appear as output for individuals outside the company. Using GenAI in a way that is not compliant with this policy may violate National September 11 Memorial & Museum at the World Trade Center’s contractual obligations with customers or vendors or, in some cases, violate applicable law.
